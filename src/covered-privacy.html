Covered

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 August 2026

Overview

Covered is an Android movement journal. It is designed to keep your movement history on your device. Covered has no user accounts, cloud sync service, advertising, or in-app analytics.

Information Covered uses

  • Location data. With your permission, Covered records approximate or precise location while you use the app. If you allow background location, it can also record location while the app is not open.
  • Physical activity information. With your permission, Covered uses Android Activity Recognition to help identify movement and manage location tracking.
  • Google Maps Timeline import. If you choose a local Timeline.json file, Covered reads route points and activity types from that file on your device. Importing is optional.
  • Derived movement data. Covered creates local sessions, route geometry, visited-street records, timestamps, distances, and app preferences from the information above.

How information is used and shared

Covered uses your data to display your routes, movement history, activity summaries, and visited streets. Location history and imported Timeline data are stored in Covered's local app database on your device.

When Covered matches a route to streets, it sends route points to the public Valhalla routing service at valhalla1.openstreetmap.de. These requests contain latitude, longitude, timestamp, and location-accuracy values needed for map matching. Covered does not upload the original Timeline file, but route points derived from an imported file may be sent for matching when Timeline street matching is enabled. The Valhalla operator receives ordinary network information, such as your IP address, as part of serving the request.

The map downloads styles and map tiles from OpenFreeMap at tiles.openfreemap.org. Tile requests can reveal the map areas you view and include ordinary network information such as your IP address.

Covered does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. We do not operate an account system or a server that stores your location history.

Retention and your choices

Covered keeps its local data until you remove it from your device. You can stop tracking or revoke location, activity-recognition, and notification permissions in Android settings at any time. Uninstalling Covered removes its local app data, subject to your device's backup settings.

Covered does not run its own backup service. If Android device backup is enabled, Android or your chosen backup provider may back up app data according to that provider's settings and policy.

Security

Local data is stored in Covered's Android app storage, which is protected by Android's app sandbox. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

Covered is not directed to children. Do not use it if you are not permitted to consent to location processing where you live.
